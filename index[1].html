Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for students and families.

How much does a lesson cost?

For this assessment prototype, the example price is RWF 5,000 per hour. Actual prices would be agreed with tutors and students.

How do online lessons take place?

The team could use Google Meet or another video-calling tool and share the link after confirming the booking.

Do I pay when I submit the form?

No. This demonstration does not collect payments. A real service would explain its payment and cancellation rules before a student books.

Are these tutors verified?

The displayed profiles are fictional examples for an assessment. A real platform should verify tutor identity, qualifications, and references before publishing profiles.
